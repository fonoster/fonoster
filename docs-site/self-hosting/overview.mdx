Self-hosting means you run Fonoster on your own machines. Your calls, recordings and data stay on
infrastructure you control, you connect your own carriers, and you sign in to your own dashboard.

## Who this section is for

This section is for the people who install and operate Fonoster: teams with on-premises or
compliance requirements, teams that already have SIP carriers, and anyone who wants full control
of the stack.

- To build agents and voice apps, go to the **Docs** tab. It applies whether you self-host or not.
- To run a private region that joins a Fonoster control plane, go to the **Private regions** tab.

## What you run

A self-hosted Fonoster runs on one machine with Docker Compose. One download and three commands
start everything a call needs:

- **The voice path**: SIP signaling, media, and the gateway that connects calls to your apps.
- **The API and the dashboard**: where you manage workspaces, agents, numbers and keys.
- **Autopilot**: the hosted runtime for your AI agents.
- **Storage**: the database, and object storage for call records and recordings.

## What isn't included

The install starts with only what a call needs. You add the rest when you want it:

- **Speech and language-model vendor keys.** Add them per workspace in the dashboard, under
  **Vendor keys**.
- **Call metrics, telemetry and private regions.** These are optional integrations. You connect
  Fonoster to your own ClickHouse, your own OpenTelemetry collector, or turn your install into a
  control plane for private regions, each with its own guide.
