A private region is a Fonoster region that runs on your own infrastructure. Calls, call records,
recordings and your provider keys stay on your machines. Your workspaces still use the same
dashboard, SDK and CLI as on any other region.

This section is for operators who run a private region. To build agents and voice apps, start at
the [Introduction](/introduction). To install Fonoster on your own servers without joining a
control plane, see [Self-hosting](/self-hosting/overview).

## How it works

A region has two parts:

- **The region** runs everything that touches a call: the voice path, Autopilot, storage and the
  provider keys. It runs on your servers with Docker Compose.
- **The control plane** is the Fonoster API and dashboard that your workspaces sign in to. It is
  [Fonoster Cloud](https://app.fonoster.ai), or your own self-hosted install.

The region connects out to the control plane with one outbound TLS connection on port `7422`.
Nothing connects in to your region over it, and call content and provider keys never leave it.
